A quiet place to pray for people near and far.

Lumen is a free prayer app. Share what's on your heart and see it land on a globe, or pray for a stranger, a friend, or a small group. No ads, no followers, no likes.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

Pray for the world

Prayers appear on a globe by place only, never with a name. Tap one, or let Lumen bring you a few that need someone.

One prayer a day

Everyone sees the same prayer each day, and you can see how many people are praying for it with you.

Pray with people you know

Add friends by code or QR and make groups for prayers that stay among you.

Hear when it's answered

When someone marks a prayer answered, everyone who prayed for it is told. Never who they are, never what it said.

Safe by design

Every prayer is checked before it appears, and anything sensitive is reviewed by a person. You can report or block anyone, edit or delete what you post, and delete your account entirely, all inside the app. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
