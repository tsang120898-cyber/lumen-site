Lumen · For the Far Away

Faith is better together.

Lumen is a spiritual community for people of faith. Share what's on your heart, stand with others in prayer, and belong to something bigger. Free, with no ads, no followers and no likes.

A community without borders

Every light is someone here with you.

A living map of the community, by place only and never with a name. Tap a glow to see what someone is carrying, and stand with them.

Today

One moment, shared by everyone.

Each day the whole community turns toward the same prayer, and you can see how many are with you. It takes a minute, and it matters.

Show up for others

A little at a time, when you can.

Have another minute? Lumen brings you five people who could use a friend in prayer, one at a time. Nothing to scroll, nothing to catch up on.

Community

Your people, your circle.

Some things are for the whole community. Some are only for the people closest to you.

Friends and groups

Add friends by code or QR, then make a group for your small group, your family or your church. What you share there stays there.

Hear when it's answered

When someone marks a prayer answered, everyone who prayed for it is told. Never who they are, and never what it said.

Your week, not a streak

A gentle row of dots shows the days you prayed. No streaks to protect, no guilt if you miss one.

Themes

Make it yours.

Six themes, from bright and crisp to candlelit. Each one changes the colors, the type and the shape of everything.

Safe by design

A gentle place to belong.

People share vulnerable things here, so safety isn't an afterthought.
